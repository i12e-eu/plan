Summary

Core Platform

i12e.eu is being developed as an independent, nonprofit reference and comparison platform for European digital infrastructure. Its shared reference covers hosted providers and open-source projects across cloud infrastructure, email, content management, payments and other core categories.

Comparisons adapt to the visitor’s role, infrastructure category and geography, making capabilities, ownership, dependencies and supporting evidence easier to assess. AI agents will maintain a public, freely reusable dataset from approved sources, with human oversight, so that people and AI tools can draw on the same reference.

Dutch Journalism

Within the SVDJ incubator on digital autonomy, the journalism application connects that reference to Dutch news organizations. Automated checks identify publicly observable dependencies; direct contact can help verify findings and add context. A personalized dependency map presents the findings as a guided story, followed by peer comparisons and relevant alternatives. Incomplete and unverified findings remain distinguishable.

Recommendations should offer a net financial benefit after accounting for the costs of changing, or cost neutrality with a substantiated sovereignty gain that the organization can communicate. These are principles for assessing change, not promised outcomes.

Delivery Sequence

Establish the core

Research, prototyping and technology experimentation began on 1 June 2026 . Main implementation began on 1 September .

Target: a first public version containing Dutch cloud infrastructure data by the end of September.

Apply and broaden

During the remaining incubator period, through November 2026, apply the reference to Dutch journalism, then extend it to further Dutch infrastructure categories. Category completion dates remain open.

Expand with funding

After the incubator, international coverage follows the Dutch phase and depends on appropriate funding.

Aim: EU funding in late 2026 or early 2027; a mature platform over roughly one year.

The funded incubator work remains within the Dutch context.
