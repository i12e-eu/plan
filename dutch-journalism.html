Dutch Journalism / Connection

Connect the reference to news organizations

The journalism stage connects maintained infrastructure information to the services used by Dutch news organizations.

Shared platform

Describes providers and open-source projects: their capabilities, ownership, dependencies, and supporting evidence.

Journalism stage

Establishes relationships between news organizations and the infrastructure they use, then links those relationships to the shared reference.

The same maintained provider record can support comparisons for several news organizations.

Dutch Journalism / Foundation

Build on a maintained foundation

Provider reference

Initial Dutch cloud coverage establishes records that help interpret organizations’ dependencies and compare relevant alternatives.

Maintained evidence

AI agents periodically examine approved websites, documentation, and company records.

Human oversight

People establish approved sources, company checks, and the criteria used to interpret the information.

Provider-data corrections enter through updated published sources and agent rescans. Incomplete or unverified information remains distinguishable.

Dutch Journalism / Dependencies

Establish the organizational dependencies

  1. Observe

    Begin with automated checks of publicly observable information about Dutch news organizations’ technology dependencies.

  2. Clarify

    Seek clarification from organizations where useful, including which comparisons are relevant to their circumstances.

  3. Connect

    Link established organization-to-provider relationships to the maintained infrastructure reference.

This is the working direction. Publicly observable information should not be presented as a complete account of an organization’s internal technology stack.

Dutch Journalism / Comparisons

Use the connection for practical comparisons

Dependency overviews
Show the infrastructure relationships that can be established for an organization.
Peer comparisons
Compare those relationships with the available information about other Dutch news organizations.
Relevant alternatives
Identify options from the shared reference and compare their documented characteristics.
Tailored guidance
Bring dependency findings, provider evidence, and peer comparisons together for an organization’s context.

The depth of comparison depends on available coverage and evidence. This use case can also help refine which information and comparisons are useful.

Dutch Journalism / Incubator

Place the work within the incubator

  1. September 2026

    Establish the reference

    Target a first public version containing Dutch cloud infrastructure data by the end of September.

  2. Remaining incubator period, through November

    Apply it to journalism

    Use the developing reference to support dependency mapping, comparisons, and relevant alternatives for Dutch news organizations.

  3. Following the journalism work

    Broaden Dutch coverage

    Extend the shared reference to further Dutch infrastructure categories. Individual completion dates remain open.

The funded incubator work stays within the Dutch context. Detailed journalism deliverables remain to be refined.

Verification depth

Detailed milestones
The journalism-specific sequence within the remaining incubator period.
